Reading the claims

A proof is always relative to a specification and assumptions. Search the specification text, filter by proof assistant or domain, and expand an entry for its theorem shape, trusted base, exclusions, and evidence.

No entries match those filters.

Inclusion rule

A concrete component or executable implementation; a Lean/Coq proof artifact; an identifiable formal contract; and an explicit trusted boundary. Verified models are labeled as models. Systems proved only with Isabelle/HOL, HOL4, Dafny, F*, Verus, or model checkers are outside this catalog’s intentionally narrow scope.
